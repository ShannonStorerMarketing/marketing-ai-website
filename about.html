About the business

Hi, I'm Shannon. I make marketing simple for small businesses.

Small business owners are experts at what they do, but most don't have the time, budget or team to keep up with digital marketing. My consulting business gives them the strategy and tools of a corporate marketing department, built around their budget and powered by AI.

Why I started this

I work as the only marketer for a mortgage branch. That means I handle everything myself: running Google Ads, auditing and updating the website, building email campaigns, editing content and reporting on results. Doing it all alone taught me how to get real results without a big team or a big budget.

Many small businesses are in the same position, just without a marketer at all. I want to bring them the same approach I use every day: clear goals, honest reporting and smart automation that saves time.

I'm also pursuing a Master of Science in AI for Business at SMU Cox. Everything I learn there, from building chatbots to automating workflows, goes straight into the services I offer.
